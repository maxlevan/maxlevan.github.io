You don’t have to explain what kept you up. You just have to admit it did.

Half Awake is ten songs I never meant to release. No rollout plan. No punchlines. Just moments that got recorded before I could clean them up.

I tracked most of it straight to tape. Not for nostalgia—just because it felt more human that way. Some vocals sit low. Some lines never land. I didn’t fix that. I didn’t want to.

This isn’t about closure. It’s about everything that lingers after you stop pretending it didn’t matter. It’s a voice memo you were never supposed to hear back.

If something in here feels familiar, I hope you keep it. If it haunts you a little, that’s fine too.

Half Awake. June 6.
